# Data Ekstraksi VLM: Industri - Kurikulum

## Bagian 1

Semester 1 berisi mata kuliah: Pancasila (2 SKS), Civics (2 SKS), Calculus 1 (3 SKS), Physics 1 (4 SKS), Chemistry (3 SKS), Engineering Drawing (3 SKS), Introduction to Industrial Engineering (2 SKS).

Semester 2 berisi mata kuliah: Introduction to Digital Transformation (2 SKS), Linear Algebra (2 SKS), Calculus 2 (3 SKS), Physics 2 (3 SKS), Laboratory Physics Experimental (1 SKS), Probability Theory (3 SKS), Engineering Mechanics (2 SKS), Engineering Materials (2 SKS).

Semester 3 berisi mata kuliah: Statistics (4 SKS), Calculus 3 (3 SKS), Computer Programming (3 SKS), Operations Research 1 (3 SKS), Manufacturing Process (3 SKS), Cost Analysis & Control (2 SKS).

Semester 4 berisi mata kuliah: Mechatronics (2 SKS), Measurement & Design of Work System (3 SKS), Organizational Behaviour (2 SKS), Operations Research 2 (3 SKS), Economics & Engineering Economics (3 SKS), Data Analytics (3 SKS), Production Planning & Control 1 (2 SKS), Industrial Ecology (2 SKS).

Semester 5 berisi mata kuliah: Ergonomics (2 SKS), Production Planning & Control 2 (3 SKS), Quality Control & Assurance (3 SKS), System Simulation (3 SKS), System Modelling (2 SKS), Industrial Organization Design & Management (3 SKS), Facilities Design (3 SKS).

Semester 6 berisi mata kuliah: Indonesian (2 SKS), Supply Chain System (2 SKS), Occupational Safety & Health (2 SKS), Product Design & Development (2 SKS), Analysis & Design of Information System (3 SKS), Business Planning (3 SKS), Research Methodology (2 SKS), Integrated Laboratory Work (2 SKS).

Semester 7 berisi mata kuliah: English 1 (2 SKS), Entrepreneurship (2 SKS), Elective Course 1 (3 SKS), Elective Course 2 (3 SKS), Integrated System Design (4 SKS), Implementation of Industrial Engineering (2 SKS).

Semester 8 berisi mata kuliah: English 2 (2 SKS), Religions (2 SKS), Final Project (6 SKS), Elective Course 3 (3 SKS), Elective Course 4 (3 SKS).

Mata kuliah pilihan yang tersedia: Marketing Management, Quality Engineering & Management, Enterprise Resources Planning System, Industrial Automation, Human Resource Management, Design of Experiment, Decision Analysis, Project Management, Meta-heuristic, Service Quality Management, Financial Management, Network Analysis, Advanced Production Planning & Control, Multivariate Analysis, Maintenance Management, Jig and Fixture & Material Handling System Design, Corporate Strategy, System Dynamics.
